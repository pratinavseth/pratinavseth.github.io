In November 2026 we ran the [Lexsi AI Safety & Alignment Hackathon](https://hackathon.lexsi.ai/), in collaboration with Cohere Labs and Hugging Face. I ran the technical side. More than 400 submissions came in.

<!-- YOUR VOICE: why Lexsi ran it, in your words: what you wanted participants to build, and what you wanted to learn. -->

## The setup

It was open to teams of one to three people, with more than $30,000 in prizes and credits. Registration opened on 12 October. A kickoff on 31 October at Polaris School of Technology in Bengaluru, with an online option, led into a build window from 1 to 15 November. Up to 20 finalist teams were announced on 18 November, with final submissions due on 24 November.

Participants built with Lexsi Labs' open-source post-training, safety and alignment stack, which I describe on the [Experience page](/experience/#systems).
