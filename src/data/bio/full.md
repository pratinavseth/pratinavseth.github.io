I lead the Model Science group at [Lexsi Labs](https://lexsi.ai/), directing research on post-training, alignment, and mechanistic interpretability. The group has grown to more than 20 researchers and interns across India and Paris, and alongside the research I handle hiring, grants, the roadmap, and getting results into the product.

**The research.** Fine-tuning, quantization, and deployment all change how a model behaves. I study which of those changes break safety, find the mechanism responsible, and test targeted ways to fix it. That thread runs through circuit-restricted refusal edits ([C-ΔΘ](/publications/c-delta-theta/)), circuit discovery and attribution ([CircuitKIT](/publications/circuitkit/)), safety-drift auditing and repair ([SafeTune](/publications/safetune/)), unlearning that survives quantization ([Forgetting That Sticks](/publications/forgetting-that-sticks/)), and alignment transfer at inference time ([ALIGNBEAM](/publications/alignbeam/)). I'm now extending it to agents and to evaluation.

**The systems.** I architected Lexsi's [alignment and safety stack](/experience/#systems): single-purpose libraries for data curation, post-training, interpretability, repair, compression, and evaluation that all write to one shared evaluation record. I scaled [AlignTune](/publications/aligntune/) into the multi-backend post-training system behind our BFSI, legal, and healthcare work. Before the LLM work, I led early design and benchmarking for the [Orion](/publications/orion-bix/) tabular foundation models and architected [TabTune](/publications/tabtune/), with follow-on work on distillation, ensembling, health data, and credit risk.

**Before Lexsi.** At Mila Quebec AI Institute I worked with David Rolnick on climate AI; my bachelor's thesis became the [Alberta Wells Dataset](/publications/alberta-wells-dataset/), a satellite benchmark for finding abandoned oil and gas wells (first author, ICML 2025). At Bosch Research I built a latent-diffusion pipeline that generated hard training examples for autonomous-driving perception. At IIT Kharagpur's KLIV Lab I worked on medical imaging and explainable AI for chest X-rays.

**As an undergraduate.** I did research on healthcare NLP at CUREYA, synthetic tabular data at Eedge.ai, malware classification at MAHE, metric learning with NEC Labs and IIT Roorkee, and agricultural NLP with Wells Fargo. I rose from trainee to lead the AI Research Wing at Mars Rover Manipal, mentoring more than ten undergraduates; co-founded The Data Alchemists; and co-led Research Society MIT Manipal, a 90-member research group.
