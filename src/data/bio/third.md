Pratinav Seth is a Lead Research Scientist at Lexsi Labs, where he leads the Model Science group's work on post-training, mechanistic interpretability, and LLM safety across India and Paris. His papers and demos have appeared at ICML, ACL, WWW, MIDL, IJCNN, and Nature Scientific Reports, with workshop and shared-task work at NeurIPS, ICLR, CVPR, MICCAI, EurIPS, EMNLP, NAACL, WACV, BioNLP, SemEval, and ACM SIGMOD. Before Lexsi Labs, he worked with Professor David Rolnick at Mila Quebec AI Institute and with Bosch Corporate Research.
