Pratinav Seth is a Lead Research Scientist at Lexsi Labs, where he leads the Model Science group across India and Paris. His research studies how fine-tuning, quantization, and deployment change the behavior of language models, and how to find and repair the circuits behind safety failures. He architected Lexsi's alignment and safety stack, including [AlignTune](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09621), [CircuitKIT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.19317), and [SafeTune](https://github.com/Lexsi-Labs/SafeTune). He has {peerReviewed} peer-reviewed papers and {citations} citations, at venues including ICML, ACL, EMNLP, WWW, and Nature Scientific Reports. Before Lexsi, he worked with David Rolnick at Mila Quebec AI Institute and at Bosch Research. He holds a B.Tech in Data Science & Engineering from Manipal Institute of Technology and was an AAAI Undergraduate Consortium Scholar (2023) and Mentor (2026).
