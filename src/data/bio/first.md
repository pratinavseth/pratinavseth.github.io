I lead the Model Science group at [Lexsi Labs](https://lexsi.ai/): more than 20 researchers and interns in India and Paris working on post-training, alignment, and mechanistic interpretability for language models.

My research asks what survives post-training. Fine-tuning, quantization, and deployment all change how a model behaves, and they can erode its safety. I look for the mechanism behind each failure and repair it in the weights: circuit-restricted refusal edits ([C-ΔΘ](/publications/c-delta-theta/)), safety-drift auditing and repair ([SafeTune](/publications/safetune/)), and alignment transfer at inference time ([ALIGNBEAM](/publications/alignbeam/)).

I also build the systems that make this repeatable. I architected Lexsi's [alignment and safety stack](/experience/#systems) and scaled AlignTune into the multi-backend post-training system behind our BFSI, legal, and healthcare work. {OssLibrariesWord} open-source libraries I designed or helped build have {stars} GitHub stars between them.

Earlier, I built generative data augmentation for autonomous-driving perception at Bosch Research and rose to lead the AI Research Wing at Mars Rover Manipal.
